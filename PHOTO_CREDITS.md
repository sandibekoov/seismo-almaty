User-facing attributions link back to the Wikimedia Commons source page.

The source pages were reviewed for what the photograph depicts and for a reusable license. Photo dates are historical and **do not establish today's appearance, access, capacity, safety, official assembly-point status or exact map coordinates**.

## How images are used

- Place listings include an actual `<img>` with a location-specific alt text and visible photo-credit link.
- The map's **assembly-point Bottom Sheet** shows a photograph of the selected landmark, the credit and the existing **not officially verified** warning.
- Photographs are **not** used for earthquake epicenters, synthetic events, automatically named hospitals or the MCHS building when an exact corresponding source photo was not verified.
- On download failure, display a text-only fallback instead of creating fictional scenery or loading an unrelated image.
- The photographs are pulled from Wikimedia; if offline or Wikimedia is inaccessible, they will not render. Storing optimized copies locally is a future option, provided original attribution remains intact.
- These images are documentary photos of landmarks, **not photographs of officially certified evacuation sites**.

## Visual quality

Display with `object-fit:cover` in modest-sized responsive cards. No aggressive grading, colorization or AI enhancements. Retain the approved light-blue Seismo UI around the original photography.
